A SIMPLE PROCESS

How it works

From camera frame to movement feedback, in a few steps.

01

Allow camera access

Your browser requests permission before capturing video.

02

Track body landmarks

Frames go over WebSocket to Python, where MediaPipe estimates pose landmarks.

03

Count movement phases

Joint angles and movement stages estimate completed repetitions.

04

Get feedback

View pose overlays, repetitions, joint angles and simple technique cues.

Prototype limitation

Thresholds are approximate. Camera angle, lighting, occlusion and positioning affect results. This is not a validated safety assessment.

About FormFit

A web-based workout assistant built with browser APIs, FastAPI, OpenCV and MediaPipe.
